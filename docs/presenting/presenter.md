# Presenter View

Presenter View shows you the current slide, the next build, your speaker notes and the time, while the audience sees
only the slides.

## Start Presenter View

Choose **Play ▸ Presenter View** (<kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>P</kbd>). Presenter View uses the animated show or
the stills, following **Play ▸ Show Animations**.

Two windows open:

- **The audience display:** the full-screen show. With a second display, such as a projector, it goes there.
- **The presenter window:** on your Mac's display. With only one display it floats above the show, so you can see
  both.

## The presenter window

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Top bar | **End Show**, **Swap Displays**, **Use Slide Show**, **Hide Slide Notes**, **Hide Build Notes** and **Feedback Mode** |
| Timer and clock | Time since the show started, and the time of day. Click the timer, or press <kbd>R</kbd>, to reset it. |
| Sidebar | Every slide, with its builds listed under it. The current slide and build are outlined. Click any build to jump to it. Slides with feedback notes have a red dot. |
| Current slide | Large. In the animated show it plays live, mirroring the audience display, with a badge saying whether the build is playing, paused or done. |
| Notes | The slide's notes and the current build's notes, side by side. |
| Counter | The slide number, with arrows to move back and forward. |

The top-bar buttons:

- **End Show** closes both windows.
- **Swap Displays** puts the show on this display and the presenter window on the other. It needs a second display.
- **Use Slide Show** closes the presenter window and keeps the show running on its own.
- **Hide Slide Notes** and **Hide Build Notes** give the other notes more room.
- **Feedback Mode** lets you pin feedback notes to the slides. See [Collecting feedback](feedback.md).

## Keys

The show's keys work in the presenter window too: <kbd>→</kbd> or a click on the current slide to advance,
<kbd>←</kbd> or a right-click to go back, <kbd>N</kbd> for the next slide, <kbd>B</kbd> to black out the audience
display, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to end. See [Keys during a show](playing.md#keys-during-a-show) for the full list.

In Presenter View, <kbd>R</kbd> resets the timer rather than replaying the build; to replay, press <kbd>←</kbd> then
<kbd>→</kbd>. <kbd>F</kbd> turns Feedback Mode on and off.

## Edit notes during the show

Click a notes panel to type into it, for example to jot down a question from the audience next to the slide it was
about. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>↩</kbd>, or click elsewhere, to save the notes and return the keys to
the show. Notes typed here are saved into the deck like notes typed in the editor.
