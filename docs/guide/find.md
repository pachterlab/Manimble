# Find and replace

Manimble searches the text of every slide in the deck, and optionally the speaker notes.

## Find text

1. Choose **Edit ▸ Find…** (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>F</kbd>), or click in the find box at the right end of the Home bar.
2. Type what you're looking for. Matches are found as you type, and the box shows how many there are.
3. Press <kbd>↩</kbd> to go to the next match and <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>↩</kbd> for the previous one, or use the arrow
   buttons beside the box, or **Edit ▸ Find Next** and **Edit ▸ Find Previous**.

Going to a match shows its slide, at the build where the text first appears, with the object selected. Every match on
the slide on screen is highlighted, and the current one more strongly.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or click **✕**, to clear the search and return to the canvas.

## Search options

The buttons inside the find box turn options on and off. An option is lit while it is on.

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Match case** | Upper and lower case must match. |
| **Whole word** | Matches only whole words. |
| **Regular expression** | The search text is a regular expression. |
| **Speaker notes** | Also searches slide and build notes. A match in the notes opens the notes tab at that build. |

## Replace text

1. Choose **Edit ▸ Find and Replace…** (<kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>F</kbd>), or click the chevron at the left of the
   find box. A replace row drops down under the Home bar.
2. Type the search text and the replacement.
3. Click **Replace** to replace the current match and move to the next, or **Replace All** to replace every match in
   the deck.

Replacements are edits like any other: the slides re-render, and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> undoes them.
